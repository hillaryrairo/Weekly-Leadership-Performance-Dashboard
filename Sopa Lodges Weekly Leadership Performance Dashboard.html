Room nights sold is derived as gross room revenue divided by ADR, computed month by month and then summed; small differences from other room-night figures in the source data are due to rounding.

EBITDA is measured against the same dedicated budget column in both years, and compared like any other metric.

2026 figures default to the actual-plus-forecast (A+F) view. All variance coloring follows one rule: green when favorable or at/above budget, red when unfavorable, grey where the comparison does not apply.

The View toggle (top right) switches 2026's headline Revenue, Occupancy, ADR, Room Nights Sold and RevPAR between A+F (blends confirmed bookings with the remaining months' forecast) and Confirmed (already-confirmed reservations only, excluding Budget and any still-provisional bookings). EBITDA, Extras Revenue and Total Gross Revenue have no Confirmed-only breakdown anywhere in the source data, so they're hidden from the KPI row, the property table, the Kenya/Tanzania split, Month Focus, and the trend chart's metric tabs whenever Confirmed view is active — switch back to A+F to see them. 2025 is a closed year with no forecast or provisional layer at all, so the toggle has no effect there.
